# Math Quiz Game

A simple math quiz game I made in C++. You choose how many questions you want, the type of operation (Add, Sub, Mul, Div, or Mix), and the difficulty level (Easy, Med, Hard, or Mix).

The program generates random questions based on your choices and checks your answers one by one, showing if you got it right or wrong (with a color change on the screen). At the end, it shows your total results and whether you passed or failed. You can also play again if you want.
